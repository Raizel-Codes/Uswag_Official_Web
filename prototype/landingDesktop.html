Article II — Constitutional Purpose

Three mandates.
One organization.

Every USWAG initiative is grounded in our founding document, ensuring our efforts outlast any single academic year or student council term.

01
Mandate 01

Youth Voice & Participation

Promoting meaningful student involvement in decision-making, governance, and civic leadership. We advocate for a real seat at the table — ensuring student perspectives shape university policies.

02
Mandate 02

Mental Health & Wellness

Advancing mental health literacy, establishing peer support systems, and leading wellness campaigns. We believe in proactive care over reactive crisis management — for every student's flourishing.

03
Mandate 03

Environmental Leadership

Leading sustainability efforts and hands-on stewardship among the student body. Treating the protection of our campus and coastal communities as a shared student responsibility.
