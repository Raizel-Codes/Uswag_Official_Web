3. SYSTEM DESIGN & PERMISSION MAPPING SUMMARY

Role Hierarchy:
  ├── Super Admin   : President / Delegated VP (Full System Control, Bylaws, Transition)
  ├── Officer Admin : Secretary, Treasurer, Auditor, Business Mgr, PRO (Scoped by Duty)
  │   ├── Secretary : Members, minutes, official announcements
  │   ├── Treasurer : Dues collection, financial statements
  │   ├── Auditor   : Audit logs, financial examination, attendance records
  │   └── PRO       : News posts, Facebook sync, advocacy content
  ├── Member        : Verified Org Family (Dashboard, Digital QR ID, RSVP, History)
  └── Public/Guest  : Unauthenticated Visitors (Public pages, Calendar, News, Join Us)

Design Tokens (Tailwind & CSS Tokens):
  ├── Deep Forest    : #142718 (Primary text, headers, footer, dark surface)
  ├── Primary Green  : #2E5B41 (Buttons, links, active navigation, section headings)
  ├── Sage Accent    : #98CBB0 (Hover states, badges, QR frame, chart accents)
  └── Mist Background: #E7EFE4 (Page background, container cards, input fields)
